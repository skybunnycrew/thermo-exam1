EFFICIENCY VS. CARNOT

Green: the cycle's η. Striped: Carnot between the same lowest and highest temperatures. Stirling and Ericsson reach Carnot only because their regenerators are perfect.

MODELS. Gas cycles: cold-air-standard, air from Table A-2a at 300 K (cp 1.005, cv 0.718 kJ/kg·K, k 1.4). Otto and Diesel use the same numbers as the Otto and Diesel page (r 9 and 17, qin 1500 kJ/kg). Brayton, the turbojet and Rankine use the numbers from the Tue Nov 24 lecture. Steam: Tables A-5 and A-6. The turbojet's η is kinetic-energy gain over heat in. Every cycle here is ideal unless it says otherwise; real devices lose more at every step.
